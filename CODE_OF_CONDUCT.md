# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in PyAgentsKit respectful, inclusive and free from
harassment, regardless of experience level, identity, background or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- communicate with empathy and technical clarity;
- critique ideas and code, not people;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them constructively;
- respect privacy, security and confidential information;
- prioritize the health of the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing and publication
of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and communication that violate
these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent exclusion.
